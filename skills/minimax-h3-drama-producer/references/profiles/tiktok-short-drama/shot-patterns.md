# TikTok short-drama shot patterns

- Hook close-up or low-angle action in the first two seconds.
- Character refusal or decision in a stable medium close-up.
- Threat or stakes framed with the relevant object or authority figure.
- Crisis with faster camera energy and motivated sound.
- Competence or evidence shown through a clear hand/object action.
- Counterattack that points screen attention toward the reveal source.
- Reveal with a lighting, framing, or sound contrast.
- Payoff reaction followed by a short hero hold.

Use exact keyframes for every shot. Keep the same character master, wardrobe, environment master, and prop ledger across all keyframes.
