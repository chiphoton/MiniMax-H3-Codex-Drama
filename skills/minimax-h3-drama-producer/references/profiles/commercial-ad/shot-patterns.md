# Commercial shot patterns

- Benefit hook with product or outcome visible immediately.
- Tactile macro that proves material, interface, or mechanism.
- Demonstration with clear before/action/after causality.
- Lifestyle or environment shot only when it supports the approved audience promise.
- Low-angle, orbit, macro glide, or controlled push-in for the product hero.
- Stable brand lockup and CTA produced in post, not by generative text.

Build a product sheet when geometry repeats. Use exact keyframes for proof, hero, package, and CTA-adjacent shots.
