# TikTok short-drama QC

Hard-fail a missing hook, escalation, payoff, main-character identity change, unreadable dialogue caption, or wrong vertical composition. Confirm that caption overlays avoid top and bottom platform zones and do not cover the active face or decisive prop.

Review the whole sequence for cause and effect: every threat must precede its consequence, object state must change only after the shown action, and the reveal source must be prepared before it appears.
