# TikTok short-drama storytelling

Open inside the problem. Avoid introductory logos, neutral establishing shots, or backstory before the hook. State the immediate power imbalance or unanswered question through action.

Preserve the user's plot. When expansion is requested, use a compact escalation chain: provocation, dignified response, stronger threat, objective crisis, competence or evidence, antagonist counterattack, payoff, consequence. The payoff may be identity, justice, skill, romance, fear, or another user-selected emotion; do not force an identity reveal into every story.

End with consequence rather than explanation. A final value line should sharpen the payoff, not lecture the viewer.
