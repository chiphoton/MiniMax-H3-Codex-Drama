# TikTok short-drama audio and captions

Enable captions by default. Use no more than two lines, strong outline or background separation, and deterministic keyword highlighting. Keep captions above platform controls and below faces or decisive hand action. A top hook may use a separate safe region.

When scripted dialogue exists, use consistent voices unless the user opts out. Build impact with motivated effects, short tension beds, silence before the reveal, and a clear payoff accent. Use an incoming sound cue to bridge into a reveal when possible.
